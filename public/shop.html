Questions

Do people need an app to see my page?

No. Your page opens in the phone’s browser when they tap your card. Most modern phones read NFC cards, and your personal link can also be shared by message.

Can I change my details after I get the card?

Yes. Log in with your account and edit your photo, details and links anytime. The card itself never needs to change.

What is the difference between Essential and Signature?

Both give you a personal page and a card with your name on it. Signature adds a matte black executive look, profile visit records, your company, how long you’ve been in business, and a list of your businesses.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Message us and we can switch your account between Essential and Signature.

How do I pay?

Choose GCash, bank transfer or cash on delivery / meetup when you order. For GCash and bank transfer you can upload a screenshot of your payment right in the form.

How long until I get my card?

We will contact you on the number you give to confirm your order and arrange delivery or meetup.
